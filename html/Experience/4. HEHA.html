This is HEHA Sky View

Popular hilltop destination that offers breathtaking panoramic city views and stunning sunset backdrops.

  • What is It?

    • Perched high up in the hills of Gunungkidul, Heha Sky View is one of Yogyakarta’s trendiest modern destinations. It is a beautifully designed hilltop park known for its spectacular panoramic views of the city below, stylish terraced cafes, and iconic, Instagram-ready photo spots.

  • Why should you go?

    • If you want the absolute best view of Jogja's skyline, this is where I’m taking you. It's the ultimate spot to relax with a cool drink, feel the refreshing mountain breeze, and snap some incredible photos on the glass skywalk as the city lights begin to twinkle below us.
