This is Merapi Lava Tour

An adventurous open-air jeep tour exploring the dramatic volcanic landscapes and historic ruins around Mount Merapi.

  • What is It?

    • Get ready for an adrenaline rush with the Merapi Lava Tour! This thrilling off-road adventure takes you on an open-top 4x4 jeep ride through the rugged, ash-covered landscapes left behind by Mount Merapi’s historic volcanic eruptions.

  • Why should you go?

    • You should definitely join this tour to witness the awe-inspiring power of nature firsthand. We will race through bumpy rivers, explore underground bunkers, and see ruined villages, all while getting a spectacular, front-row view of one of the world's most active volcanoes.
