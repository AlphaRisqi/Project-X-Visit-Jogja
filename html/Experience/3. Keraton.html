This is Keraton of Yogyakarta

The grand royal palace of Yogyakarta, preserving centuries of living Javanese history and elegant traditions.

  • What is It?

    • Let’s explore the royal roots of the city at Keraton Yogyakarta, the grand palace complex that serves as the official seat of the reigning Sultan. This living museum is a masterpiece of traditional Javanese architecture, preserved perfectly to showcase centuries of royal heritage.

  • Why should you go?

    • This is your exclusive pass to see real Javanese royalty and culture in action. As we stroll through the serene courtyards, you'll get to see priceless historical artifacts, watch live traditional dance performances, and feel the deep spiritual atmosphere that still guides the people of Jogja today.
