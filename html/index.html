FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION

When is the best time of year to
visit Yogyakarta?

When is the best time of year to visit Yogyakarta?

The best time to visit Jogja is during the dry season, which runs from May to October. The weather is sunny and clear, making it absolutely perfect for exploring outdoor attractions like Prambanan Temple, Borobudur, and the Merapi Lava Tour without worrying about rain.
How many days do I need to fully experience Jogja?

How many days do I need to fully experience Jogja?

To truly catch the essence of the city, a 3 to 4-day itinerary is ideal. This gives us enough time to cover the cultural highlights like the Keraton and Malioboro, spend a day temple-hopping, and still have time for an adventurous jeep tour at Mount Merapi or a relaxing sunset at Heha Sky View.
What is the best way to get around
the city?

What is the best way to get around the city?

For ultimate comfort and flexibility, renting a private car with a driver (like me!) is the best option, especially for reaching far spots like the hills or beaches. For short distances within the city center, trying out traditional transportation like an andong (horse carriage) or a becak (pedicab) is a fun, authentic experience you shouldn't miss!
Is Yogyakarta a safe destination for solo and international travelers?

Is Yogyakarta a safe destination for solo and international travelers?

Absolutely! Jogja is widely known as one of the friendliest and safest cities in Indonesia. The locals are famously warm, polite, and always willing to help. As with any tourist destination, just practice standard precautions—keep an eye on your personal belongings in crowded areas like Malioboro.
What local dishes should I absolutely try while I'm here?

What local dishes should I absolutely try while I'm here?

You cannot leave Jogja without trying Gudeg, a sweet and savory jackfruit stew cooked in coconut milk, usually served with chicken and rice. If you have a sweet tooth, make sure to try Bakpia Pathok, a delicious flaky pastry filled with mung bean or chocolate that also makes the perfect souvenir!
What should I wear when visiting religious or cultural sites?

What should I wear when visiting religious or cultural sites?

When visiting sacred places like the Keraton (Sultan's Palace) or Prambanan Temple, it is respectful to dress modestly. It is best to wear clothes that cover your shoulders and knees. Loose, breathable clothing is highly recommended anyway, as it keeps you cool and comfortable in the tropical weather!
